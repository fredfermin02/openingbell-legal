## Plus, billing and refunds

Opening Bell Plus is sold and billed by Apple, so Apple handles the money:

- **Manage or cancel:** Settings → Opening Bell Plus → Manage subscription,
  or directly at <https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions>.
- **Refunds:** Apple decides refunds, at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.
- **Plus missing after a reinstall or a new phone:** Settings → Opening
  Bell Plus → Restore purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the same
  Apple ID that bought it.
- **Free trial:** the annual plan starts with seven free days. Cancel
  before they end and nothing is charged.

## Alerts not arriving

Open Settings → Market hours & alerts and check the switches, then check
that Opening Bell is allowed to notify you in iOS Settings →
Notifications. Alerts follow the time zone the app detected; if you
travelled, the market keeps your home zone for a day before it moves.

## Reporting a fund or an exchange

Open the fund's page (Market › Book › the fund) and tap **Report**, or on
an exchange's page tap **Report** under the standings. Pick a reason; a
note is optional, and you can block the fund in the same step. Reports
come to us, never to the person reported, and we review them within 24
hours. If something can't wait, email the address above.

## Your data

- **Export:** Settings → Account → Export my data (JSON). Plus adds
  Export my statement (CSV).
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Account → Delete account & history.
  Everything is removed from our servers. An active Plus subscription is billed by
  Apple and is not cancelled by deleting the account, so cancel it in
  your Apple ID subscription settings as well.
